Hit “Start camera”, then get into a push-up position with your whole body in frame.

Space bar logs a rep by hand if the camera misses one. Each detected rep is sent straight to the server, so the OBS overlay counts down live.

Detection settings

Do one slow rep while watching the live elbow angle, then set the thresholds just inside the range you actually hit. Saved on this machine.

Feedback while you lift

You're facing the floor, so audio is the only feedback you'll actually get. The beep confirms the rep landed; the voice reads out how many you have to go.

Camera placement

Off to your side, chest height, 2–3 m back, whole body in frame from hands to feet. Angles come from the model's 3D landmarks, so a head-on camera also works — side-on is just the most reliable.
