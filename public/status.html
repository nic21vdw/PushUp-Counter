The OBS browser source

One source is the whole tracker: your camera with the pose skeleton on it, and the count beside it, on a transparent background so it composites over your scene. Add it at 1100×340, and leave “Shutdown source when not visible” switched off — the camera has to stay open while you are doing push-ups off-scene.

Give it its own webcam — not one OBS has as a Video Capture Device, or they will fight over it. Pick which with ?camera=NAME; any part of the name works.

To check your framing and tune the detector, open the setup view — it adds a live readout of your elbow angle under the picture. Close it before you go live: two pages counting would bank every push-up twice.
